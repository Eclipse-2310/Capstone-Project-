Terms of Demonstration

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Purpose of the System

The "E-Commerce Dispatch and Inventory Management" system is designed to demonstrate key Computer Science concepts:

  • Data Structures: Stack (cart management and LIFO undo), Queue (FIFO warehouse dispatch line), Singly Linked List (order lifecycle milestones).
  • Algorithms: Merge Sort (catalog price ordering in O(n log n)), Binary Search (product ID lookup in O(log n)), and Linear Substring Search.
  • Database Management Systems: 3NF Relational Schema, ACID SQL Transactions, Foreign Key Constraints, and Database Migrations.

2. No Real Commercial Transactions

Product listings, prices, inventory counts, and order checkouts are simulated demonstrations. No real currency is charged, no physical hardware will be dispatched, and no payment gateway integration exists.

3. Order Lifecycle & Cancellation Policy

In accordance with realistic warehouse logistics logic, orders may be cancelled by the placing user only while in the Placed milestone. Once an order is processed into Packed, Dispatched, or Delivered, it represents physical material handling and cannot be cancelled.
